About

How to see what has been invisible? This question led to YXiZ—pronounced “exis,” echoing existence, while containing “XYZ,” the coordinates of space and the language of mathematics. The name itself embodies our philosophy: YXiZ Lab is not built merely for profit, but for understanding — for exploring the deep structure of intelligence.

Co-founded by Yvette (Shiyi) Chen and Yujing Wang in 2025, YXiZ Lab began from a simple observation: people live years with subtle spinal misalignments they can’t see—until pain forces them to act. The lab’s first creation thus uses intelligent posture detection to help people visualize their spinal curvature clearly, track progress, and regain harmony with their own body.

By framing itself as a “lab,” YXiZ lives on a grander scheme of things beyond a tech brand. It’s a philosophy of observation—of learning to see what the eye alone cannot. We aim to bring intelligence to the act of self-understanding, transforming insights in computer vision, generative AI, mathematics, and philosophy into active creation.
